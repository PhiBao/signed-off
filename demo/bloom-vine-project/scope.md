# Statement of Work — Bloom & Vine Florist Website

**Client:** Bloom & Vine (Sarah Whitmore)
**Contractor:** Studio Fern
**Fee:** £6,400 · Milestone 2 of 3 (£2,200) — Storefront & Checkout

## Background

Sarah currently takes orders over the phone and via Instagram DMs. She loses
orders on weekends because nobody is at the shop to answer. The new site needs
to take orders without her involvement.

## Scope for this milestone

The storefront and checkout must work end to end. Specifically:

1. A visitor can browse the bouquet catalogue and see the price of every bouquet
   before adding anything to the basket.
2. A visitor can add a bouquet to the basket, and the basket total updates to
   reflect what they added.
3. A visitor can complete checkout as a guest — we agreed no account is
   required, because Sarah's customers are mostly one-off buyers.
4. Delivery date selection: the customer must not be able to choose a Sunday,
   since Sarah does not deliver on Sundays.
5. After a successful order the customer sees an order confirmation with an
   order number, and Sarah receives the order by email.
6. If a bouquet is out of stock it must not be addable to the basket.
7. The whole flow above must work on a phone. Sarah says most of her customers
   are on Instagram on their phones.

## Out of scope for this milestone

Account creation, wholesale pricing, the blog, and the loyalty scheme are
milestone 3.

## Acceptance

Sarah will sign off milestone 2 once she can place a test order herself and
receive the confirmation email.
